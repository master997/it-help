# Meeting room screens

> Room names, network names and URLs below are placeholders — swap them for the real ones.

Every room has the same kit: a screen, a USB-C cable on the table, a camera
above the screen, and an iPad by the door.

## I plugged in and the screen is black

Work down the list — it's almost always step 1 or 2.

1. **Check the screen is awake.** Tap the iPad by the door. If the screen was
   asleep it takes about ten seconds to come up. Wait for it before assuming
   the cable is the problem.
2. **Push the cable in properly.** The USB-C connector needs a firm push — a
   loose one gives you power but no picture, so a charging laptop is not proof
   it's seated.
3. **Try the other end.** Unplug from your laptop, count to five, plug back in.
   This fixes it more often than it should.
4. **Check you're not on the wrong input.** On the iPad, tap **Share screen**.
   If it's already highlighted, tap it off and on again.
5. **Try the spare cable.** Each room has a second cable in the drawer under
   the screen. If that works, leave the broken one on the table and tell us —
   don't put it back in the drawer.

Still black after all five: move rooms if people are waiting, then post in
#it-help with the room name. Getting the room name saves us ten minutes.

## The screen works but there's no sound

Your Mac is still playing sound through its own speakers.

1. Hold **Option** and click the speaker icon in the top-right of your screen.
2. Under **Output Device**, choose the room's screen — it'll be named after
   the room, e.g. **Boardroom**.
3. If the room isn't listed, unplug the cable and plug it back in, then check
   again.

If you're in a video call, do this too: in Zoom or Google Meet, click the arrow
next to the microphone icon and set **Speaker** to the room as well. The Mac
setting and the call setting are separate, which is why sound sometimes works
in music but not in the meeting.

## I can't see the room camera in Zoom or Google Meet

The camera is a separate device from the screen and has to be picked manually.

1. In **Zoom**: click the arrow next to the video icon → choose the camera
   named after the room.
2. In **Google Meet**: click the three dots → **Settings** → **Video** →
   choose the room camera.
3. If the room camera isn't in the list, your laptop hasn't seen it yet.
   Unplug the USB-C cable, wait five seconds, plug it back in, then check
   again.
4. Still not there — quit Zoom or your browser completely and reopen it. Some
   apps only look for cameras when they start.

## Someone else is showing and I need to swap

You don't need them to unplug first.

1. Plug your own cable in.
2. On the iPad, tap **Share screen**. It switches to you.

If the room has two cables on the table, both can stay plugged in — the iPad
decides who's showing.

## The room iPad is frozen

1. Hold the button on the top edge until you see the power slider, drag it
   across, wait ten seconds, then hold the same button to switch it back on.
   It takes about a minute to come back.
2. While it restarts, you can still share by plugging in directly — the screen
   usually switches on its own.
3. If it comes back and still won't respond, post in #it-help with the room
   name and carry on with the meeting. Don't unplug anything behind the screen.

## Nothing here worked and the meeting starts in two minutes

Move to another room if one's free, or dial in from your laptop with headphones
and share your screen that way. Fix it after, not during — post in #it-help
with the room name and what you tried, and we'll get to it before the next
booking.
