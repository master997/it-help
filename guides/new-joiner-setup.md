# New joiner: setting up your Mac

> App names, tool names and URLs below are placeholders — swap them for the real ones.

Welcome. This should take about 45 minutes. You don't need to finish it all
before your first meeting — get through "day one" and come back to the rest.

## Day one: what to do first

Do these four in order. Each one depends on the last.

1. **Turn the Mac on and follow the setup screens.** When it asks you to sign
   in, use your work Google account (the `@company.com` one in your welcome
   email), not a personal Apple ID.
2. **Set a password you'll remember.** You'll type this several times a day.
   It must be at least 12 characters. A short phrase with a number in it works
   well and is easier to live with than something random.
3. **Connect to Wi-Fi.** Click the Wi-Fi icon top-right and join **Office-5G**.
   The password is on the card in your welcome pack, or ask anyone sitting near
   you — it's not a secret.
4. **Open Company Portal** (it's already installed, in your Applications
   folder). Sign in with your work account. This is what installs everything
   else, so nothing below will work until you've done this.

Once Company Portal says "You're all set", the standard apps start installing
by themselves. It takes about twenty minutes. You can keep using the laptop
while it happens.

## How do I get the apps I need?

Open **Company Portal** and browse the list. Click **Install** on anything you
need — no approval, no ticket, no waiting.

You'll get these automatically without asking: Chrome, Slack, 1Password, Zoom,
Google Drive, Notion.

## The app I need for my job isn't in the list

Some tools are paid per-person, so they're not installed by default. Post in
#it-help with the app name and one line on what you need it for. That line
isn't a test — it's so we buy the right licence tier.

Don't download it from the internet yourself, even if there's a free version.
That's how two people end up on different versions and nothing lines up.

## Setting up 1Password

1Password stores every work password so you only have to remember your Mac one.

1. Open **1Password** and choose **Sign in with your work account**.
2. Accept the invite in your email if it asks for one.
3. Install the browser extension when it offers — this is the part that makes
   it actually useful, because it fills passwords in for you.

Everything shared with the whole company lives in the **Everyone** vault: the
Wi-Fi password, the printer, shared logins.

## I don't have access to something I need

Access isn't automatic for everything — some tools are granted per team.

Post in #it-help with the tool name and what you're trying to do. Most things
are granted the same day.

If it's during your first week and something's obviously missing (no email, no
Slack, no calendar), that's a setup problem rather than a permissions one —
say so and we'll treat it as urgent.

## Something's gone wrong and I'm stuck

You won't break anything by asking. Post in #it-help with:

- what you were trying to do
- what happened instead
- a screenshot if there's an error message

That's genuinely all we need. New-joiner setup problems are common and usually
five minutes to fix.
